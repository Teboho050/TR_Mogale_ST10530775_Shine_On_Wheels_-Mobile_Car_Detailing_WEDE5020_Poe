About Us

Learn about the story, mission and vision behind Shine On Wheels Mobile Car Detailing.

Our Story

Shine On Wheels Mobile Car Detailing was established in 2026 by Teboho Mogale in Johannesburg, South Africa.

The business was created to provide convenient, high-quality mobile car detailing by travelling directly to customers' residences, workplaces or other agreed locations.

Our services have grown to include interior and exterior detailing, ceramic coating, paint protection and engine bay cleaning.

Our Mission

To offer professional, convenient and affordable mobile car detailing services for vehicle owners, with outstanding customer service, attention to detail and high-quality products.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and preferred mobile car detailing services in South Africa, growing into more cities while maintaining quality, convenience and dependable vehicle care.
