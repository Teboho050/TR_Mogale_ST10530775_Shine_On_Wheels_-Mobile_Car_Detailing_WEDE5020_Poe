##PROJECT OVERVIEW

Shine on Wheels Mobile Car Detailing was established in 2026 by Teboho Mogale in Johannesburg, South Africa. The enterprise was created to offer convenient, high-quality mobile car detailing services by traveling directly to the clients' residences, workplaces, or other designated locations. Since its inception, the business has broadened its array of services to include interior and exterior detailing, ceramic coating, paint protection, and engine bay cleaning, while cultivating a reputation for dependable service and customer satisfaction.  


##WEBSITE GOALS AND OVERVIEW:

Enhance the Online Presence of the Business 
The primary objective of the website is to create a professional online presence for Shine on Wheels Mobile Car Detailing. This platform will facilitate easier access for potential customers to locate the business via search engines, gain insight into the services provided, and retrieve essential information such as contact details, operating hours, and service locations. 
 
•	Enhance Customer Inquiries and Online Reservations 
The second objective is to offer customers an simple and useful method for inquiring about services and making online appointments. By integrating an inquiry form and a booking request feature, customers will have the ability to arrange mobile detailing services at their convenience, without the necessity of making phone calls or sending messages via social media 
 
•	Fostering Trust and Highlighting Our Services 
Our third objective is to highlight the exceptional quality and professionalism of our business. Our website will feature in-depth descriptions of our services, pricing details, before-and-after photos, customer testimonials, and answers to frequently asked questions. By providing this information, we aim to instill confidence in our customers, attract new clientele, and enhance our reputation in the local community. 

 KPS:•	Enhance Online Inquiries 
 
In the initial three months following the website launch, the business aims to obtain a minimum of 30 online inquiries per month via the website's inquiry form. This objective will serve as an indicator of the website's effectiveness in attracting potential customers and motivating them to seek additional information regarding the services offered. 
 
•	Growing Our Online Bookings 
 
Our goal is to secure a minimum of 20 online booking requests each month during the first three months after we launch our website. This key performance indicator will help us device how well the site is turning visitors into paying customers by offering an easy and enjoyable booking experience. 
 
•	Increase Website Traffic 
 
The objective of the website is to draw in a minimum of 500 unique visitors per month during the initial three months following its launch. Website traffic will be assessed utilizing tools such as Google Analytics to evaluate whether the website effectively engages its target audience and enhances the online visibility of the business
